Privacy Policy

For games, applications, and services published by Omrano The Scorpion Dev.

Last updated: July 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Omrano The Scorpion Dev (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use our games, mobile applications, websites, and related services (collectively, the “Services”).

1. Information We Collect

We aim to collect as little personal information as possible. Our Services may collect limited information automatically, such as device type, operating system version, language, approximate location derived from IP address, app version, crash reports, and anonymous gameplay or usage events. This information helps us operate, secure, and improve the Services.

We do not intentionally collect names, postal addresses, phone numbers, or precise location unless a particular Service clearly tells you otherwise and obtains any required consent. If you contact us, we collect the information you choose to include in your message and our correspondence with you.

Depending on the Service, these can include Google Play Services, Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase, crash-reporting tools, and platform storefronts. These providers may process device identifiers, IP address, diagnostic data, usage data, and ad-interaction data under their own privacy policies.

Google Play Services may support essential app functions. AdMob may show ads and measure their performance; where required, it will request consent for personalized advertising. Analytics tools help us understand aggregate use of the Services. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party provider used by a specific Service.

3. Advertising and Analytics Choices

Where a Service includes advertising, you may see personalized or non-personalized ads depending on your settings, region, and consent choices. You can manage ad personalization through your device settings and, when provided, the in-app privacy or consent options. Choosing not to receive personalized ads does not necessarily stop all advertising.

4. App Permissions and Device Data

Our apps request only permissions needed for their stated features. For example, storage or media access may be used to save user-created content, notifications may be used for reminders you enable, and network access may be used for online features, ads, updates, or analytics. We do not access a permission-controlled feature unless you grant permission. You can review or revoke permissions at any time in your device settings; some features may then be unavailable.

5. How We Use Information

We use information to provide and maintain the Services, troubleshoot errors, improve gameplay and functionality, protect against fraud or abuse, measure advertising and analytics where enabled, respond to support requests, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Data Sharing

We may share limited information with service providers that help us deliver the Services, such as analytics, advertising, hosting, crash reporting, and distribution partners. We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transfer. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to meet legal requirements, or to resolve disputes. Anonymous or aggregated data may be kept longer. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you by emailing omraneelitdev@gmail.com. We may need to verify your request and may retain certain information where legally required.

If a Service stores your data locally on your device, uninstalling the app or clearing its storage will generally remove that local data. Store purchases and cloud-backed data may be governed by the relevant platform’s policies.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under the age at which parental consent is required in their country, unless a specific Service states otherwise. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.
