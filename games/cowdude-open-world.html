Cowdude:

A first-person Western comedy sandbox. Wander into Dustville, start trouble, and find out how far the sheriff's posse will chase you before you lose them — or end up in the cell.

Explore, cause chaos, get chased, escape.

An open frontier of nine areas around the town of Dustville — a gun shop, a clothing store, a livery, a jail that actually holds you, and a stagecoach that goes somewhere whether or not anyone is watching.

  • Ride horses, rob the stagecoach, and run bounties across the badlands
  • Wanted stars, a chasing sheriff's posse, and a jail with three ways out — serve it, pay bail, or kick the door
  • Buy guns and upgrades, and dress the part with hats, bandanas and masks that double as a disguise
  • Built for Android in landscape, with full touch controls and a cheat-code menu
